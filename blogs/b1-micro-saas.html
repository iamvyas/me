What is mirco SAAS ?

Most software fails not because it lacks features, but because it tries to solve too many problems at once. MicroSaaS products take the opposite approach. They focus on one specific pain point, for a very specific group of users, and solve it exceptionally well. Our product was born out of that philosophy. We noticed a recurring friction in small teams: important tasks were getting lost, not because people were careless, but because existing tools were too heavy, too noisy, or too generic for day-to-day workflows. Instead of building yet another all-in-one platform, we stripped everything down to what actually matters.

The tool does one thing: it helps teams capture, track, and close small but critical tasks before they turn into problems. No complex onboarding. No endless configuration screens. You open it, add what needs attention, and move on with your work. This simplicity isn’t accidental — it’s intentional. Every additional feature increases cognitive load. By keeping the surface area small, the product stays fast, predictable, and easy to trust.
